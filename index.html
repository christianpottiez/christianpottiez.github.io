About

I’m interested in the point where understanding people starts changing products.

My background spans market research, UX research and product development, giving me a broad methodological toolkit across qualitative and quantitative approaches.

I’m driven by curiosity about what people actually do — not only what they say they will do — and by turning that understanding into better product decisions.

I take a progressive but grounded approach to new methods. Rather than following every trend, I evaluate emerging approaches early, pilot them in practice and scale those that demonstrate real value.

I care deeply about research quality, but I don’t equate quality with maximum rigour in every situation. Sometimes a fast, directional answer is what a team needs; in other cases, robust data and high methodological precision are essential. Knowing which standard a decision requires — and designing the research accordingly — is a key part of how I work.

Today, much of my work focuses on scaling research: making access easier, improving the underlying infrastructure and finding pragmatic ways to give researchers more time for interpretation and impact.
