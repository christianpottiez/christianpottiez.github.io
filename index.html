About

I’m interested in the point where understanding people starts changing products.

My background spans market research, UX research and product development. What I enjoy most is not research for its own sake, but creating the conditions for better decisions — whether that means challenging an assumption about a customer journey, building a research capability that works across product teams, or redesigning a workflow with AI.

Working across both market and UX research has shaped how I look at customer behaviour: not as an isolated usability question, but as part of a broader decision context. I’m equally comfortable discussing study design and evidence as I am working with product teams on what an insight actually means for the experience.

Today, much of my work focuses on scaling Research: making access easier, improving the underlying infrastructure and finding pragmatic ways to give researchers more time for interpretation and impact.
