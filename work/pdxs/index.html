04PDXS

PDXS

A flexible in-house measurement programme for customer-facing digital touchpoints.

The survey programme regularly assesses digital touchpoints and provides product teams with a basis for prioritising improvements.

Customer-facing digital touchpoints (examples)
Porsche.com
My Porsche App
Online Shop
Car Configurator
Car Finder
My roleQuestionnaire logic, dimensions, fixes, weighting, stakeholder alignment, technical rollout, reporting concept and quality control.
Study objectiveCreate a regular health check for digital touchpoints and a continuous basis for prioritization.
Program contextIn-house measurement across customer-facing touchpoints; regular analysis including Germany and the US.
01
The challenge

The existing study was too rigid for the product teams.

An external agency study provided general findings, but its questionnaire could not be adapted well to product-specific questions.

Before

Agency study

  • High external costs
  • Not adaptable
  • Weak fit for our digital products
→
After

In-house program (PDXS)

  • Lower external costs
  • Flexible and adaptable
  • Tailored to our digital products
02
Program design

A standardised core with flexible modules.

The programme follows an 80/20 structure: a stable core allows comparisons across touchpoints, while flexible modules cover team-specific questions and current topics.

80%

Standardised core

  • Recurring dimensions
  • Comparable metrics
  • Cross-touchpoint benchmarking
20%

Flexible module

  • Team-specific questions
  • Current topics
  • Wave-by-wave adaptation
A comparable core, with room for questions that change between waves.
03
What PDXS measures

A regular assessment of digital touchpoints.

The survey records why people visit, whether they achieve their goals and where they encounter problems across customer-facing touchpoints.

Visit intentWhy people come
  • Intent to visit again
  • Product interest
  • Specific tasks or needs
Goal completionWhether they achieve their intent
  • Task success
  • Partial success
  • Drop-off
ProblemsWhere friction occurs
  • Usability issues
  • Content gaps
  • Technical problems
Usability ratingOverall usability scale
  • Overall design rating
  • Ease of use
  • Clarity and navigation
Teams used the findings to set priorities for product improvements.

05
How teams work with PDXS

How teams use the results.

Product teams use the same set of results to identify priorities, agree on measures and track subsequent changes.

1. Review resultsReview key metrics and findings in the dashboard.
2. Use filtersAnalyse by touchpoint, market, wave or device.
3. Identify prioritiesWorkshops define the most important topics.
4. Define actionsReview findings internally and agree measures.
5. Track improvementsMonitor progress in subsequent waves.
