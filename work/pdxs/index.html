04PDXS

PDXS

From a rigid external study to a flexible in-house measurement program for customer-facing digital touchpoints.

An in-house survey program created to continuously assess customer-facing digital touchpoints and provide an ongoing decision basis for improvements.

Customer-facing digital touchpoints (examples)
Porsche.com
My Porsche App
Online Shop
Car Configurator
Car Finder
My roleQuestionnaire logic, dimensions, fixes, weighting, stakeholder alignment, technical rollout, reporting concept and quality control.
Study objectiveCreate a regular health check for digital touchpoints and a continuous basis for prioritization.
Program contextIn-house measurement across customer-facing touchpoints; regular analysis including Germany and the US.
01
The challenge

The existing study was too rigid for our digital products.

An external agency study provided general insights, but the questionnaire was not adaptable and had a weak fit to our product-specific questions.

Before

Agency study

  • High external costs
  • Not adaptable
  • Weak fit for our digital products
→
After

In-house program (PDXS)

  • Lower external costs
  • Flexible and adaptable
  • Tailored to our digital products
02
Program design

A standardized core with flexible modules.

The program follows an 80/20 logic: a stable core for comparability across touchpoints, combined with flexible modules for team-specific questions and current topics.

80%

Standardized core

  • Recurring dimensions
  • Comparable metrics
  • Cross-touchpoint benchmarking
20%

Flexible module

  • Team-specific questions
  • Current topics
  • Wave-by-wave adaptation
One program. Comparable insights. Flexible enough for what’s next.
03
What PDXS measures

A general check of digital touchpoints.

We capture why people visit, whether they achieve their goals, and where they encounter problems — across all customer-facing touchpoints.

Visit intentWhy people come
  • Intent to visit again
  • Product interest
  • Specific tasks or needs
Goal completionWhether they achieve their intent
  • Task success
  • Partial success
  • Drop-off
ProblemsWhere friction occurs
  • Usability issues
  • Content gaps
  • Technical problems
Usability ratingOverall usability scale
  • Overall design rating
  • Ease of use
  • Clarity and navigation
Insights were prioritised with teams and translated into product improvements.

05
How teams work with PDXS

From insights to action.

PDXS provides a shared and trusted basis for data-driven decisions. Product teams use the results to prioritise, align and track improvements.

1. Review resultsExplore key metrics and insights in the dashboard.
2. Use filtersAnalyse by touchpoint, market, wave or device.
3. Identify prioritiesWorkshops define the most important topics.
4. Define actionsReview findings internally and agree measures.
5. Track improvementsMonitor progress in subsequent waves.
