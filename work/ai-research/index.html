03AI-enabled Research Workflow

Developing an AI-supported Research Workflow

I design and refine a workflow that applies AI support where it creates the most leverage — reducing repetitive work and manual processing — so more time can be invested in interpretation, deep dives and overall research quality.

My role

  • Workflow architect
  • Prompt design
  • Research methodology
  • Implementation
  • Training & adoption

AI support areas

  • Briefing
  • Questionnaire / guide creation
  • Quantitative analysis
  • Reporting preparation

Impact

  • Faster drafting
  • Faster quantitative analysis
  • More time for interpretation

Principle

AI support removes repetitive work, while researchers remain responsible for quality, methodology and interpretation.
