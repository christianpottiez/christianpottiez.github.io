03 · AI Transformation

Developing an AI-supported Research Workflow

A workflow concept that integrates AI support into selected parts of Research — removing repetitive work and creating more time for interpretation and deeper analysis.

My roleWorkflow concept · prompt design · research methodology · pilot development
Core principleAI-assisted execution · researcher-led interpretation and quality
Strongest proofQuantitative analysis workflow in Copilot for Excel
01 · Opportunity

AI created new leverage inside an already established Research process.

The starting point was not a broken or inefficient process. New AI capabilities simply made it possible to remove repetitive work and reuse context across research phases.

I designed the target workflow and the reusable prompts for quantitative analysis, with a clear principle: AI supports selected steps, while researchers retain responsibility for methodological decisions, interpretation and communication.

02 · Lifecycle

AI support is selective — not end-to-end automation.

Briefing, research design, questionnaire / discussion-guide drafting and analysis can benefit from AI support. Programming remains a classic workflow, while interpretation and communication remain researcher-led.

03 · Briefing & Study Design

Reuse context instead of recreating it.

A Teams briefing can be recorded and transcribed, then structured into the existing research briefing template — including research context, scope, hypotheses, timing and other relevant information.

Because that context already exists in the LLM, it can accelerate research-design thinking and generate a first draft for a questionnaire or discussion guide. The researcher then iterates on that draft several times before the instrument is final.

Pilot in development

A questionnaire agent is being piloted to apply a defined framework of questionnaire “golden rules”, such as scale construction and other methodological conventions.

04 · Quantitative Analysis

The strongest current use case is repetitive analysis preparation.

Instead of manually preparing labels, syntax, descriptive tables and cross-tabs in a traditional statistics workflow, reusable prompts in Copilot for Excel can generate inspectable, formula-based analyses directly in the workbook.

~2 days

Typical quantitative analysis effort in my previous workflow.

~0.5 day

Current analysis effort in my own application of the AI-supported workflow.

The goal is not only speed.

The time gained can be reinvested into interpretation, deeper dives and additional analyses — improving the quality of the final research output.

05 · Quality by Design

Use AI to create transparent analysis logic.

For quantitative analysis, AI is used to create formulas and analysis logic that remain visible in Excel. This is preferable to relying on opaque generated numerical answers.

Traceable to source dataInspectable formulasResearcher validation
06 · Supporting Tools

The workflow is broader than Excel Copilot.

Copilot in Excel

Formula-based descriptive analysis, cross-tabs and chart preparation.

Copilot / LLM context

Structured briefing documentation and first drafts for study materials.

Condens

AI-enabled transcription, anonymisation and qualitative repository capabilities that complement researcher-led synthesis.

The qualitative workflow is intentionally presented as a supporting capability rather than as the core transformation case.

07 · Demo

Show the workflow with synthetic data.

A short screen recording can demonstrate the quantitative workflow without exposing company or customer data: paste a reusable prompt into an example dataset, generate the analysis logic, create a table and chart, then move the output into the presentation format.
