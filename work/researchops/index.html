01ResearchOps

Building a scalable Research Practice

An operating model for continuous product research across several teams.

I worked on how research was sourced, staffed and conducted across six digital products. The resulting setup combined dedicated research capacity, easier participant access and a consolidated tool stack, reducing coordination effort and making research easier to run.

02
The challenge

The existing setup did not support ongoing research.

Research itself was valued, but the operating model was fragmented. Commissioning, tooling and participant access required considerable time and budget.

Fragmented commissioning

Research demand was handled case by case, with new sourcing and repeated onboarding for each project.

  • Individual sourcing per project
  • Repeated agency selection
  • Limited continuity across studies
Result: high coordination effort and low scalability

Fragmented tooling

Different business areas commissioned tools independently, creating overlap and reducing purchasing leverage.

  • Limited overview of existing tools
  • Overlapping solutions across teams
  • Weak negotiation position
Result: unnecessary costs and inefficient tool usage

Slow and costly participant access

Recruitment relied mainly on CRM-based access and external crowd panels, making studies slower and less precise.

  • Customer access mainly via CRM
  • Long and costly recruiting cycles
  • Lower participant fit in crowd-based recruiting
Result: slower studies and inconsistent participant quality
High friction   ·   High cost   ·   Slow access   ·   Limited continuity

Capacity

Building continuity and product knowledge.

The model developed from ad-hoc agency assignments to a pool of researchers and then to dedicated researchers with a product focus.

  • Low continuity

  • Generic product knowledge
→

Researcher Pool

→

Dedicated Researcher

  • Deep product knowledge
  • High continuity
  • Strong collaboration
I designed the operating model, established the external research setup and defined how researchers work with product teams.

Employees

  • Very fast
  • Low cost

Porsche Advisors Club

  • Existing customer community
  • Direct customer access
  • Reusable participant base
  • High relevance

Digital Intercepts

  • Customers & prospects in context
  • Reduced recruitment costs

Research teams no longer had to start participant recruitment from zero for every study.

Managing the tool stack centrally.
