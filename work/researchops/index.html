01 · ResearchOps

Building a scalable Research Practice

From fragmented, project-by-project research to a scalable operating model for continuous product research.

01 · Challenge

Research worked — but it did not scale efficiently.

Research requests originally triggered individual sourcing processes, changing agencies and repeated onboarding. Product teams had to re-explain context, participant recruitment often started from zero, and the tool landscape grew over time.

The opportunity was not to invent a new research method, but to redesign the operating model around continuity, access and reusable infrastructure.

02 · System

I treated ResearchOps as a system, not a collection of tools.

The operating model was built around three connected levers: recurring research capacity, easier participant access and an actively managed research infrastructure.

03 · Capacity

From changing agencies to recurring product expertise.

I established framework contracts so research capacity could be accessed when needed without starting a full sourcing process each time. The model then evolved further: researchers increasingly worked repeatedly with the same digital products.

This reduced briefing effort and allowed product knowledge to accumulate over time instead of being rebuilt project by project.

04 · Participant Access

Recruitment became a reusable capability.

Employee pool

Pragmatic access for quick-and-dirty testing and rapid iterations.

Porsche Advisors Club

A reusable customer community for repeated access to relevant participants.

Digital intercept recruiting

Recruitment directly on customer-facing touchpoints to reach customers and prospects in context.

05 · Tool Strategy

The tool stack became an actively managed portfolio.

I introduced an annual review of research tooling: actual usage, overlapping functionality, new market capabilities and future needs. This created a deliberate tool portfolio rather than a collection that simply grew over time.
