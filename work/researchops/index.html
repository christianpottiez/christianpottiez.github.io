01 · ResearchOps

Building a scalable Research Practice

From fragmented, project-by-project research to a scalable operating model for continuous product research.

01 · Challenge

Research worked — but it did not scale efficiently.

Research was already established across the organisation, but the underlying operating model was fragmented. Three recurring challenges made studies slower, more expensive and harder to scale.

Sourcing

Research requests were handled as individual projects. Agencies changed frequently, resulting in repeated sourcing and onboarding. Teams had to explain the product context again for each project, slowing down delivery and limiting continuity between studies.

Participant access

Research teams could recruit either existing customers or participants through crowd-testing platforms, but both routes had limitations. Recruiting customers was costly and time-consuming, while crowd testers did not always provide the required participant fit and data quality. Neither option offered an efficient, reliable and repeatable way to access the right participants.

The opportunity was not to introduce a new research method, but to redesign the operating model around continuity, access and reusable infrastructure.

Tooling

Product teams commissioned research tools independently, without a shared overview of the wider tool stack. This resulted in overlapping contracts in some areas, capability gaps in others and limited transparency around costs, ownership and usage.

02 · Sourcing

From changing agencies to recurring product expertise.

I established framework contracts so research capacity could be accessed when needed without starting a full sourcing process each time. The model then evolved further: researchers increasingly worked repeatedly with the same digital products.

This reduced briefing effort and allowed product knowledge to accumulate over time instead of being rebuilt project by project.

03 · Participant Access

Recruitment became a reusable capability.

Employee pool

Pragmatic access for quick-and-dirty testing and rapid iterations.

Porsche Advisors Club

A reusable customer community for repeated access to relevant participants.

Digital intercept recruiting

Recruitment directly on customer-facing touchpoints to reach customers and prospects in context.

04 · Tooling

The tool stack became an actively managed portfolio.

I centralised ownership of the research tool stack and introduced an annual portfolio review covering actual usage, overlapping functionality, emerging market capabilities and future needs. This created a deliberately managed tool portfolio rather than a collection that simply grew over time.
