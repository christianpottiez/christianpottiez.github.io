01ResearchOps

Building a scalable Research Practice

From fragmented, project-by-project research to a scalable operating model for continuous product research.

I transformed how research was sourced, staffed and executed across six digital products. By establishing dedicated research capacity, easier participant access and a consolidated tool stack, I reduced operational friction and increased the impact of research.

04
Capacity

From agencies to embedded expertise.

We evolved the model from ad-hoc agency assignments to a pool of researchers, and finally to dedicated researchers with product focus.

Ad-hoc agency

  • Low continuity
  • Repeated onboarding
  • Generic product knowledge
→

Researcher Pool

  • Flexible access
  • Less onboarding
  • Growing product knowledge
→

Dedicated Researcher

  • Deep product knowledge
  • High continuity
  • Strong collaboration
I designed the operating model, established the external research setup and defined how researchers work with product teams.
05
Participant access

Removing recruitment as a bottleneck.

Three complementary channels give us fast and cost-efficient access to the right participants.

Employees

  • Internal participant pool
  • Very fast
  • Low cost
  • Ideal for quick tests

Porsche Advisors Club

  • Existing customer community
  • Direct customer access
  • Reusable participant base
  • High relevance

Digital Intercepts

  • Recruiting on digital touchpoints
  • Customers & prospects in context
  • Reduced recruitment costs
  • Improved participant quality
Research teams no longer had to start participant recruitment from zero for every study.

Infrastructure

Treating the tool stack as a portfolio.

I centralised ownership of the research tool stack and introduced an annual review to ensure we use the right tools, avoid duplication and stay up to date with the market.
